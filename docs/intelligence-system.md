# **PROJECT: WEALTH LAB — YOUR VOICE, YOUR EXPERIENCE**

## **1\. YOUR ROLE**

Act as a senior brand strategist, premium UX/UI designer, customer experience researcher, behavioural researcher, conversion-focused form architect, and experienced frontend developer.

Your assignment is to design and implement a sophisticated, interactive, multi-step feedback and expectation form for Wealth Lab.

This is not a conventional survey.

It is a strategic community intelligence initiative designed to help Wealth Lab understand its previous attendees, uncover their experiences and challenges, identify their interests, and use those insights to curate a more thoughtful, relevant, and premium experience for the next edition.

You must approach this project with the understanding that Wealth Lab is an exclusive community, not a generic event or mass-market conference.

The finished experience should communicate exclusivity, sophistication, intentionality, belonging, and genuine interest in the members' opinions.

## **2\. THE PROBLEM**

Wealth Lab is already working on plans, strategies, programme structures, potential facilitators, and exciting initiatives for its next edition.

However, there is an important gap.

Much of the planning and ideation has been happening internally, without sufficiently involving the people the experience is ultimately designed for: the community members.

We risk designing an impressive programme based on our own assumptions without adequately understanding:

* What members actually experienced at the previous edition.  
* What they enjoyed and found valuable.  
* What disappointed them or could have been better.  
* What logistical difficulties they encountered.  
* What they would like to experience differently.  
* What knowledge, perspectives, and practical guidance they are looking for.  
* What would make them more likely to attend again.  
* What kind of experience they consider worth investing in.

We must address this gap before finalising the next edition.

## **3\. THE SOLUTION**

Create a premium, engaging, multi-step community feedback experience titled:

**YOUR VOICE. YOUR INTERESTS. YOUR WEALTH LAB EXPERIENCE.**

Supporting message:

"Your Voice Matters. Help Us Shape What's Next."

The form should make previous attendees feel valued, heard, and involved in the evolution of Wealth Lab.

We want to understand the community before making important decisions about the next edition.

The intelligence gathered should inform:

1. Experience design and event planning.  
2. Venue selection and accessibility.  
3. Transportation and accommodation considerations.  
4. Programme structure, duration, and scheduling.  
5. Facilitator and subject-matter curation.  
6. Health, Purpose, Power, and Wealth content priorities.  
7. Hospitality and premium experience expectations.  
8. Ticket categories and access preferences.  
9. Community engagement and networking opportunities.  
10. Attendance projections and future planning.

The ultimate objective is to use real community intelligence to create a more relevant, intentional, and premium Wealth Lab experience.

## **7\. ANONYMITY AND PRIVACY**

Anonymity must be an intentional and clearly explained feature.

At the beginning of the form, ask:

"How would you like to share your feedback?"

Options:

* With my identity.  
* Anonymously.

If the member selects anonymous:

* Do not display name, email, or phone-number fields.  
* Do not require personally identifying information in subsequent steps.  
* Do not include identifying information in submission metadata or hidden fields.  
* Do not accidentally collect their identity through optional questions.  
* Allow honest criticism without fear of association.

If the member selects identified:

Collect only:

* Full name.  
* Email address (optional, unless genuinely required for a specific follow-up).

Explain that their information will be used only for feedback analysis and relevant community communication.

Do not promise complete technical anonymity if the form provider or hosting infrastructure retains IP addresses or other metadata. Verify the actual data-collection behaviour and disclose relevant limitations.
