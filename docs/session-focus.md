SESSIONS & FACILITATORS FOR THE WEALTH LAB

Where Wealth Builders Think, Build, and Execute.

1. Financial Intelligence & Wealth Investment

Understanding Money. Building Assets. Securing the Future.

Facilitator: Chief Bola Kolawole

The objective of this session is to develop financially intelligent individuals who understand that wealth creation goes beyond making money today. It involves deliberately structuring, growing, protecting, and sustaining wealth over a lifetime.

Participants should develop an understanding of how to:

Build wealth through deliberate financial decisions.
Invest in shares, property, funds, and other productive assets.
Understand investment risks and opportunities within the Nigerian and global economies.
Structure their finances for long-term growth.
Make informed decisions regarding taxes, savings, and investments.
Prepare for retirement long before retirement approaches.
Build retirement assets and independent sources of income.
Protect their financial future and avoid financial dependence in old age.

The Philosophy

Retirement should not be a period of financial helplessness that people simply hope to survive. It should be a season they deliberately prepare for, beginning from their productive years.

We are not teaching people to wait until old age before thinking about financial survival. We are teaching them to build a future in which they retain their dignity, choices, financial independence, and quality of life.

2. Health, Protection & Longevity

Protecting Your Life. Preserving Your Health. Extending Your Capacity.

This area covers two complementary dimensions and will require two facilitators for each dimension, except where a single facilitator has expertise across both.

A. Life Insurance & Financial Protection

Key areas of focus include:

Understanding life insurance and its importance.
Types of life insurance policies.
Protecting dependants and family interests.
Financial protection against unexpected life events.
Choosing appropriate insurance coverage.
Understanding insurance as an essential component of responsible wealth management.
B. Personal Health & Preventive Living

Key areas of focus include:

Nutrition and healthy eating.
Understanding food choices and their long-term consequences.
Common diseases and lifestyle-related health conditions.
Recognizing symptoms and knowing when to seek medical attention.
Preventive healthcare and routine health checks.
Healthy diets, practical recipes, and sustainable eating habits.
Maintaining physical and mental capacity as one grows older.

The Philosophy

Wealth is not particularly useful if the person who created it lacks the health to enjoy it.

3. The Culture of Influence, Etiquette & Elite Conduct

The Royal Art of Conduct, Communication, Relationships & Access.

Facilitator: Mrs Kola Bola

Many people come from humble backgrounds and may have limited exposure to the social norms, cultural expectations, and protocols associated with influential environments, even as they begin to experience financial stability and freedom.

This session seeks to expose participants to the social intelligence, cultural awareness, communication standards, and relationship skills required to navigate such environments confidently and appropriately.

The session will explore four key areas:

A. Ethics, Etiquette & Personal Conduct
Social and professional etiquette.
Executive presence and personal comportment.
Dressing appropriately for different environments.
Dining etiquette and social protocols.
Emotional intelligence, discretion, and confidentiality.
Understanding the difference between confidence and arrogance.
B. Communication & Public Speaking
Articulation and eloquence.
Public speaking and presentation.
Conversational intelligence.
Active listening and asking intelligent questions.
Communicating with authority without intimidation.
Written and professional communication.
C. Relationships, Networks & High-Value Circles
Building meaningful relationships with influential people.
Interacting with high-net-worth individuals (HNIs).
Understanding relationship dynamics and social capital.
Networking without appearing transactional.
Maintaining relationships with discretion and integrity.
Understanding how trust, reputation, and credibility influence access.
D. The Culture of the Palace (The Courtiers)
Understanding palace culture and traditional protocols.
The conduct expected in the presence of royalty and dignitaries.
Respect, hierarchy, discretion, and service.
Understanding the role of courtiers and trusted attendants.
Navigating privileged spaces without losing one's identity or dignity.

The Philosophy

Access is one thing. Knowing how to conduct yourself when you gain access is another.

You can enter a room through an invitation, but your character, intelligence, conduct, and ability to relate determine how you are received and remembered.

INTERACTIVE EXPERIENCES
1. Panel Session & Interactive Q&A

A Closed-Room Conversation. Shared Perspectives. Direct Engagement.

The program will feature panel discussions that allow participants to engage directly with speakers and experienced professionals.

Rather than relying solely on conventional presentations, the panel format will encourage:

Multiple perspectives on relevant issues.
Honest conversations around practical challenges.
Questions and contributions from participants.
Direct engagement with panelists.
The exchange of ideas, experiences, and actionable insights.

Objective

To create an environment where knowledge is not merely delivered from the stage but also questioned, discussed, and collectively explored.

2. The Wealth Lab Hot Seat

Real Businesses. Real Challenges. Meaningful Intervention.

The Hot Seat will spotlight one or two participants: an entrepreneur and a 9–5 professional.

One participant will be invited to present their business, respond to questions, and openly discuss their business challenges, opportunities, and immediate needs.

The individual will be examined through a practical case-study approach, with participants, speakers, and relevant professionals contributing perspectives, recommendations, and possible solutions.

Where possible, the exercise will extend beyond advice to tangible support through relevant connections, expertise, partnerships, and resources.

Objective

To move beyond discussing business success in theory and identify real businesses that can benefit from the collective intelligence and resources within the room.

3. Collaborative Challenge — The Three Circles

Think Together. Solve Problems. Present Solutions.

Participants will be divided into three collaborative circles, with each team assigned a practical scenario or problem to solve.

The scenarios may cover areas such as:

Financial management and investment.
Corporate leadership and organisational challenges.
Entrepreneurship and business growth.
Strategic decision-making.
Other relevant business and wealth-building challenges.

Each team will deliberate, develop its approach, and nominate one representative to present its proposed solution.

The presentations will be assessed, and the winning team will receive collective gifts.

Objective

To encourage collaborative intelligence, critical thinking, teamwork, problem-solving, and the ability to communicate ideas under pressure.

The exercise transforms participants from passive listeners into active contributors.

4. Brand Reputation & Authority

Facilitator: Taiwo Faruq

Taifaq Pixels · Crescend · Uprix

Proposed Session Title: Building an Authoritative Brand in the Age of AI

This session will explore how individuals and businesses can build credible, recognisable, and influential brands in an increasingly competitive and AI-driven world.

Key Areas of Focus

Branding in the Age of AI

Understanding how artificial intelligence is changing branding, visibility, content creation, and market positioning.

How Not to Brand

Identifying common branding mistakes, weak positioning, inconsistent communication, and practices that undermine credibility.

Building Brand Authority

Establishing a recognisable voice, strong positioning, trust, and authority in a competitive market.

Building Loyal Communities

Moving beyond followers and impressions to cultivate genuine relationships, engagement, and brand loyalty.

Reputation Management

Understanding how public perception, consistency, customer experience, and credibility shape a brand's long-term value.

Practical Component: Brand Assessment

The session should extend beyond theoretical discussions. Participants should have the opportunity to assess their existing brands, identify weaknesses, and receive practical recommendations for improvement.

Desired Outcome

Participants should leave with a clearer understanding of how their brands are perceived, what needs to change, and how to build stronger brand authority.
